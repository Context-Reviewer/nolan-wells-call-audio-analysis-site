Why captions matter

Ambiguous audio

Compression, distance, and noise suppression can erase or distort phonetic detail.

Caption-induced priming

When words appear before listening, the brain may fit ambiguous sound to the suggested phrase. Once formed, the interpretation can become difficult to unhear.

Method and limitations

Method

  1. Preserve the downloaded stream.
  2. Create a lossless working copy.
  3. Inspect codec and channel structure.
  4. Separate the two channels.
  5. Apply conservative, non-generative filtering.
  6. Compare raw and processed versions.
  7. Use blind listening to reduce priming.

Limitations

  • The source was transcoded through YouTube.
  • The native MDMR file has not been obtained.
  • Filtering cannot restore discarded information.
  • Aggressive processing can create speech-like artifacts.
  • Repeated listening increases expectation effects.
No generative speech reconstruction was used to manufacture, complete, or replace missing words.
