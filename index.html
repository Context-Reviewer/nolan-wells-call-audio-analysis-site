Why captions matter


    

        

            
Ambiguous audio

            

                Weak recordings contain incomplete phonetic information.
                Compression, noise reduction and distance can erase or distort
                important consonants.
            

        


        

            
Caption-induced priming

            

                When suggested words are displayed before listening, the brain
                may fit ambiguous sounds to the written phrase. Once formed,
                that interpretation can become difficult to unhear.

Method


    
    
        
  1. Preserve the downloaded YouTube stream unchanged.
    2. 
        
  2. Create a lossless working copy.
    3. 
        
  3. Examine codec, channels, duration and signal structure.
    4. 
        
  4. Separate the two recorded channels.
    5. 
        
  5. Review the raw microphone-side channel.
    6. 
        
  6. Apply conservative, non-generative filtering.
    7. 
        
  7. Compare interpretations across raw and processed versions.
    8. 
        
  8. Listen before revealing proposed wording to reduce caption-induced priming.
    9. 
    


    

        No language model or generative speech reconstruction was used to
        manufacture, complete or replace missing words.
    






    
Limitations


    
    
        
  • The working source was transcoded through YouTube.
    • 
        
  • The native MDMR file has not been obtained.
    • 
        
  • Filtering cannot restore information discarded before upload.
    • 
        
  • Aggressive processing may create or exaggerate speech-like artifacts.
    • 
        
  • Repeated listening can increase expectation and pattern completion.
    • 
        
  • Perceived wording does not independently establish conduct or intent.
