Formal methods

Scope, procedure, and quality controls

The rules governing the independent review, including what has been completed, what remains provisional, and what cannot be established from a YouTube-derived recording.

This framework improves documentation and reproducibility. It does not convert the project into an accredited laboratory examination.

Status

Controls now in place

Phase 1 documented

Defined scope

The examination question, source, candidate intervals, permitted conclusions, and excluded claims are fixed in writing.

Evidence manifest

Repository audio files are inventoried with SHA-256 hashes, byte sizes, media metadata, and source limitations.

Standard procedure

Raw listening precedes processing and wording prompts. Processing must remain disclosed, conservative, and directly comparable with the source.

Confidence framework

Technical findings and speech interpretations use different criteria. Existing speech conclusions remain provisional until rescored and independently reviewed.

Observation separation

Acoustic observations, free responses, proposed wording, alternatives, model output, and confidence decisions are recorded in separate fields.

Change and review controls

Method or conclusion changes require a recorded reason. Independent review has a defined checklist and remains explicitly incomplete until performed.

Current limits

  • The native CXone/MDMR export and its custody records have not been obtained.
  • Browser audio-engine validation against known signals is the next internal quality-control phase.
  • Filter-residue playback and localized cross-channel measurements remain to be implemented.
  • Controlled blind-listening data and independent technical review require people other than the initial examiner.
